How the system works

01
Detection. An accelerometer measures impact force and lean angle, and a vibration sensor detects shock. A crash triggers on high G-force, a sustained tilt past 45 degrees, or repeated heavy vibration.
02
Confirmation. A buzzer sounds for 20 seconds. If the rider is conscious they press the button and the alert is cancelled.
03
Alert. With no response, the location and time go to the family Telegram group with ride booking links, and as a push notification.
04
Zone. That spot becomes an accident prone zone for the duration set above.
05
Route warning. Plan a journey and any zone along the road is flagged before you leave. While navigating, the app speaks a warning at 1 km, 500 m and 200 m before each zone.
Sound and voice. Tap Start monitoring on the home page once. Phones block audio until you do. Routing uses the free OSRM service and place search uses OpenStreetMap. No paid service is used anywhere in this app.
